Format of the catalogue lines (engine-dev#369 pack v1):
  path | title | author | source URL | licence
Paths use the publisher-tree names from MANIFEST.json (and the static exercise paths).
They document the work; they do not have to equal KOLIBRI_DATA/content/storage/… paths.
The licence column includes the licence name and version, the licence URL where one
applies, and a transcoded or “unchanged (no derivatives)” note where that applies.

Standard attribution sentences for the licences used in this file are collected
after the catalogue. Use the per-work title, author and source URL together with
the sentence for that work’s licence.

Kolibri packaging notes
- This file covers the works inside the Kolibri demo pack (export v4 plus CREDITS).
- Still images are packaged for Kolibri as a small html5 zip (the original image plus
  a short index.html wrapper from the IDEA channel builder). That wrapper is IDEA
  tooling, not a separate third-party work. The licence below is for the image.
- The three channel databases stamp author “IDEA Demo” and a generic channel-level
  licence (CC BY-SA on Early Reading and Grade 5 Science; CC BY-NC-SA on Maths) on
  every resource. Those stamps are not the publisher licences. The per-work entries
  below are the ones that apply.
- Commons videos (prepare=video in fetch.py) are transcoded to H.264/AAC, scaled
  to at most 1280 px wide, and trimmed to at most 90 seconds; a silent audio track
  is added if the original had none. Khan Academy videos (prepare=video-full) are
  transcoded the same way but are not trimmed. Their licence is Creative Commons
  Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 United States (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 US),
  taken from section 7.2.2 of the Khan Academy Terms of Service snapshot dated
  31 December 2023 at https://web.archive.org/web/20231231210525/https://www.khanacademy.org/about/tos
  (the live TOS page could not be read when these credits were prepared).
- ND (no derivatives) works are included unchanged.
- Export v4 added three Storybooks UK PDFs that were not in v3: Magozwe,
  Grandma’s bananas, and The Honeyguide’s revenge. This credits file is for v4.
